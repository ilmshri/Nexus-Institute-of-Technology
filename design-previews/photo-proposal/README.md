# Photography proposal assets (design session, 2026-07-31)

Delivery-ready renditions of the six licence-verified Wikimedia Commons
photographs used in the review gallery, plus navy-duotone variants
(greyscale remapped into the site's own #14294B -> #FDFCFB ramp — zero
new colour). meta.json carries per-photo source page, licence, credit and
original resolution; weights.json the measured KB per rendition.

Captions must stay honest ("Representative example: ..."); CC BY-SA images
require visible credit, and the duotone remap is an adaptation (share-alike
applies to the adapted image). Nothing here ships until the owner chooses.
