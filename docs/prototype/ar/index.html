01The MechEd standards

Three standards govern everything we publish.

Mastery

Every lesson is genuinely learnable from zero — full derivations, worked arithmetic that is machine-verified before it is published, and honest depth labels on anything still in production.

Integrity

No fabricated references, no invented industrial data, no marketing numbers. Representative figures say so — plainly.

Innovation

Industry 4.0 as core engineering, not a footnote — PLCs, SCADA, IIoT and digital twins woven through the classical spine.
