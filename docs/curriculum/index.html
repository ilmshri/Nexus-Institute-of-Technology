CURRICULUM4 years · 8 semesters

48 courses. 528 lessons. One pathway.

The complete B.S.-shaped map — from foundational mathematics and physics to Industry 4.0 — covering every core mechanical-engineering discipline and role. Every lesson already has its own page — scope, sources, and checkpoint questions — and full teaching depth is rolling out course by course, tracked honestly below. The catalog is a strict grid — 6 classes per semester, 11 lessons per class, 528 lessons — built as authored content, never as padding.

254 of 528 lessons at full teaching depth · 48% — updated with every build. The rest are in production, in queue order.

Full depth = complete lecture + foundations + five-problem interactive quiz (or a Tier-1 lecture / Core-60 study guide).

Depth you can audit. Every claim on this page is recomputed from the content at build time.
