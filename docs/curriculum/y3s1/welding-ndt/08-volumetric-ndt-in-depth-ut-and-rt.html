WLD 305 · Lesson 08 of 11 · Year 3 · Semester 1

Volumetric NDT in depth: UT and RT

Ultrasonic testing: wave propagation, probes, calibration blocks, and angle-beam weld scanning — sizing and characterizing indications, where UT skill actually lives. Radiographic testing: source selection, geometry, image quality indicators, and film and digital reading of the classic defect appearances. Radiation safety as an absolute boundary.
